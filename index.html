Zareen means golden. We took it less as a colour than as a measure — the quiet worth of a thing made slowly, owned for years, and never spoken of loudly.

We began with one question: what would a hoodie look like if it were treated like fine tailoring? Not louder, not branded, not seasonal — but considered to the last stitch, warm against the skin, and the same friend a decade from now.

The answer became a single garment, refined obsessively rather than multiplied carelessly. We don't release collections. We release one piece, and we make it as well as it can be made.

The making

Three quiet
obsessions.

01 — Cloth

A cashmere hand

Spun from a blend of Mongolian cashmere and long-staple Supima cotton, the loopback knit is brushed to a softness that holds warmth without weight. It settles into you, not the other way around.

02 — Hand

Finished by people

Seams are felled by hand, cuffs are set with a tonal stitch, and the drawcord is tipped in solid brass that warms to a soft gold over time. Each piece passes through nine pairs of hands.

03 — Time

Made to outlast

Pre-washed for a calm, settled drape and reinforced where wear gathers. Cared for simply, it is meant to be the piece you reach for in ten winters — and the one you mend rather than replace.
